Components

Copy-into-app components for Native Shell, built on nsh/base.

83 components, one page each: Installation, Usage, an optional Composition, Examples and API. They are grouped in two: Base, aligned with an NSH element, and Enhanced, this library's additions with no NSH element of their own. Behavior, keyboard support and the full unstyled props live on nsh.dev/elements; each page links its base element and the shadcn/ui component it stands in for.

The list is generated from registry.json and these pages, so every registry:ui item has a page and every page has an item. The group comes from

Coverage: a page registered in one of its NSH rows is Base, one in none is Enhanced. Add one with npx shadcn-nsh@latest add <name>.

Base

  • Accordion — List rows that reveal panels with a turning chevron.
  • Alert Dialog — A confirmation of the platform's own.
  • Avatar — A round picture or name-colored initials on the identity ring.
  • Button — A button, an icon button, a round button, and a split button.
  • Calendar — A month of days to pick a date or a range, on the shared calendar look.
  • Carousel — Snapping slides with arrows and a traveling dot pill.
  • Checkbox — A sunk box that fills in citrus, with a check or a dash springing in.
  • Collapsible — A disclosure row that reveals a panel beneath it.
  • ColorPicker — Area, sliders, joined fields, swatches, and a popover trigger.
  • Combobox — A searchable field with a list of choices and chips for the values chosen, on the shared listbox look.
  • Command — A search palette with sectioned, virtualized rows.
  • Context Menu — A menu at the pointer, on the shared menu look.
  • DataTable — A striped table with sortable, resizable columns.
  • Date Picker — A calendar in a popup from a field trigger, on the shared listbox look.
  • Dialog — A card dialog with a trigger, content, title, description, footer and close.
  • DiffView — Up and down tints for additions and deletions, with hunk actions.
  • DockArea — Dockable workspace chrome for tabs, splits, and edge docks.
  • Editor — An editor on the field's well, with line numbers, diagnostics and a find bar.
  • Emoji picker — Tabs, search, skin tones and grid styled for nsh/base EmojiPicker.
  • Hover Card — A preview card that grows from its trigger, on the shared popover surface.
  • Input — A text field — sunk, ghost, multiline, masked, or invalid.
  • Link — A text link with underline motion, on the shared nav look.
  • Menu — A menu of items, separators and submenus on the shared menu look.
  • NavStack — Pages pushed on a stack — the system header on a phone, the app's own themed header on a desktop.
  • Number Input — A sunk number field with − / + or stacked chevron steppers.
  • OTP Input — One-time code cells that lift out of sunk wells as digits fill in.
  • Pagination — Page numbers and chevrons with the current page lit primary.
  • Plot — Chart colors, grid, cross line and tooltip for nsh/base/plot.
  • Popover — A popup panel that grows from its trigger, on the shared menu surface.
  • Pressable — A region that takes presses and adds only their feedback.
  • Progress — A determinate or indeterminate progress bar.
  • Radio — A sunk ring that fills in citrus, with a dot growing in — alone or as a choice tile.
  • Resizable — Resize handles with a quiet hairline and a growing thumb.
  • Rich Editor — Rich document typography and its session API, on nsh/base's RichEditor.
  • Scrollbar — A hover scroll bar in the shared gallery theme.
  • Select — A list of choices from a field trigger, on the shared listbox look.
  • Sheet — A panel that slides in from an edge of the screen over a scrim.
  • Slider — A sunk rail, citrus fill, and round thumb with a focus ring.
  • SplitView — Two or three columns of navigation stacks, with the column header and the sidebar button in the theme's look.
  • Switch — An on / off switch whose knob springs across a sunk track.
  • Table — Composition parts for a bordered table card using the shared data-table classes.
  • Tabs — A pill track or underlined row with a traveling indicator.
  • Terminal — A program's output on the theme's field surface, the keys typed back to it.
  • TextView — Brand-colored links and a pulsing streaming caret.
  • Time Field — A sunk well for a time of day, one segment at a time.
  • TitleBar — The bar that drags a desktop window, the window buttons a frameless one draws for itself, and the edges that resize it.
  • Toast — A raised banner with a title, optional description, action and close.
  • Toggle — A two-state button, in two variants and three sizes, and a group of them.
  • Toolbar — An inset track of tools, with groups and hairline separators.
  • Tooltip — A popover-coloured hint bubble on any element's `tooltip` prop.
  • Tree — A virtualized tree with chevrons and folder icons.

Enhanced

  • Alert — A notice of the whole page or form, read out when it appears.
  • Aspect ratio — A box as wide as its column and as tall as its ratio says.
  • Attachment — A file, a picture or a link carried by a message: its media, its name, how it is doing, and what can be done to it.
  • Badge — A small capsule label in a neutral, toned, or outline style.
  • Breadcrumb — The trail from where the app starts to the page it shows.
  • Bubble — The framed surface of one turn of a conversation, and the reactions that hang off it.
  • Button group — Buttons joined into one control, in a row or a column.
  • Card — A flat surface that groups related content: a header with its action, a body and a footer.
  • CodeBlock — Code, a command's output or a log in a block, with a title, a copy button, line numbers and a footer.
  • CollapsingHeader — A page's header that shrinks into a bar as the page scrolls, its picture sliding under the title.
  • Drawer — A panel from the bottom edge, with a grabber, that a swipe closes.
  • Empty — A centered block that says there is nothing here yet, and what to do about it.
  • Field — A set of fields under a legend: a label, its control, a description and errors that are read out.
  • ImageViewer — One image full screen over a dimmed ground, fitted, with pinch to zoom, drag to pan and swipe down to close.
  • InputGroup — A field's frame around an input and what sits beside it — an icon, a unit, a button.
  • Item — A row of a list: what sits before its content, a title with a note, and controls after it.
  • Kbd — A key of a shortcut, alone or in a group.
  • Label — The visible name of a control, beside it or over a group of them.
  • Marker — A line in a conversation that is not a message: a status, a note, a labeled separator.
  • Menubar — A row of menus whose triggers stand in one bar, as a desktop app's menu bar.
  • Message — One row of a conversation: the sender's avatar, who they are, the turn, and what is under it.
  • Message scroller — A conversation that keeps up with itself: new turns follow the newest message while you are at the end, and wait when you are not.
  • Navigation Menu — A row of triggers, each opening a panel of anything under the bar — one at a time.
  • RefreshIndicator — The round chip a pull to refresh draws over a scroll view, traveling in with the pull and spinning while it refreshes.
  • Segmented — A track of mutually exclusive choices whose thumb springs to the chosen one.
  • Separator — A hairline across the content, or down a row.
  • Sidebar — An app's navigation column beside its page — folded to a strip of icons, or sliding in as a sheet on a phone.
  • Skeleton — The shape of content that is still coming.
  • Spinner — A turning ring: work going on.
  • Stat — A label above a value for metric grids.
  • Status — A dot and label for quiet state in rows or headers.
  • Trace — An agent's steps, one line each — reasoning, a tool call, a source, a failed step — where a line can open onto what it did.
